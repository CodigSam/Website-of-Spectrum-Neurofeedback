About Us

We Learn Smart Way To Build Bright Futute For Your Children

Neurofeedback, also known as EEG biofeedback, is a non-invasive therapeutic technique that helps individuals train their brain activity to achieve better self-regulation. Using sensors placed on the scalp, it monitors brainwave patterns and provides real-time feedback through visual, auditory, or tactile cues. This training encourages the brain to optimize its function, offering potential benefits for conditions such as ADHD, anxiety, depression, sleep disorders, and migraines. While research supports its effectiveness for some applications, neurofeedback outcomes can vary, and consistent sessions guided by a trained professional are typically required for lasting results.

Changing brainwaves necessitates a reasonable amount of effort. Maintaining concentration during neurofeedback training can be particularly challenging for children with special needs. This is the point where therapists can have a significant impact. Our therapists possess extensive experience working with children who have special needs. We ensure that the child remains motivated and engaged throughout the training. Consequently, the ideal degree of advancement can be accomplished.

We also observed that individuals who work hard to earn points generally progress more quickly than those who lack the motivation to score. At Spectrum Neurofeedback, in-house training, we create various engaging games, videos, and music to maintain the children's interest. As emphasized earlier, when the child strives to participate and improve, we observe better outcomes and advancement.

Finally, our therapist charts your child's advancements into graphs following each session's conclusion. The therapist will then talk to you about any advancements or declines in the brainwaves that are being trained. In this manner, parents remain well informed regarding their child's advancement.

What We Do

QEEG Brain Mapping

QEEG Brain Mapping is a technique used to pinpoint crucial regions of the brain for brain training or Neurofeedback Training. The QEEG charts an individual's brainwave patterns as they perform particular activities, including reading, answering comprehension questions, solving math problems (addition, mental math, and problem-solving), composing text, and listening to both a classical piece and a pop song. The concept is to assess the EEG (brainwaves) while an individual is engaged in a task. For children, we will converse with the parents regarding which tasks they are most worried about. Through QEEG Brain Mapping, we can identify brain regions that are functioning at their best, are inactive, and those that experience fatigue while executing the task. Utilizing this data, a distinctive brain exercise (Neurofeedback) program can be developed. At Spectrum Learning, the QEEG analysis will serve as a framework to create training protocols for the brain exercise (Neurofeedback) training and for evaluation after training. This results in more efficient training and guarantees that particular goals are achieved.

Wearing the QEEG cap

The QEEG cap resembles a swimming cap and fits snugly on the head. It features 20 electrodes positioned in accordance with the "International 10-20 System of Electrode Montage." This electrode cap simultaneously records the electrical signals or brain waves (EEG) produced by the brain at 19 different points on the scalp. It is extremely secure since it only detects what the brain emits. Nothing is released into the head.

Applying the QEEG gel

A unique QEEG gel is administered to each electrode with a syringe. This aims to create a strong connection with the scalp for precise readings of the EEG or brain waves.

Checking for contact problem and any impedance issue

The consultant will evaluate the contact quality from each electrode with a specific meter. If the contact quality is poor, the consultant will modify the electrodes until a good quality contact with the scalp is achieved.

Recording the brainwaves

The client will be requested to complete several tasks, each lasting 3-5 minutes, while their brainwaves are recorded. The two standard tasks involve keeping the eyes open while sitting relaxed and keeping the eyes closed while sitting relaxed. Subsequently, different cognitive tasks will be assigned. The assignments provided are based on the client's requirements. Instances consist of reading a text, answering comprehension questions, solving math problems (addition, mental sums, and word problems), composing an essay, and listening to both a classical piece and a pop song. As the client participates in each task, brainwave patterns are captured from 20 locations at the same time.

Have Questions?

Neurofeedback addresses problems of brain deregulation. These happen to be numerous. They include the anxiety-depression spectrum, attention deficits, behaviour disorders, various sleep disorders, headaches and migraines, PMS and emotional disturbances. It is also useful for organic brain conditions such as seizures, the autism spectrum, and cerebral palsy.

We apply electrodes to the scalp to listen in on brainwave activity. We process the signal by computer, and we extract information about certain key brainwave frequencies. (All brainwave frequencies are equal, but some are more equal than others….) We show the ebb and flow of this activity back to the person, who attempts to change the activity level. Some frequencies we wish to promote. Others we wish to diminish. We present this information to the person in the form of a video game. The person is effectively playing the video game with his or her brain. Eventually the brainwave activity is "shaped" toward more desirable, more regulated performance. The frequencies we target, and the specific locations on the scalp where we listen in on the brain, are specific to the conditions we are trying to address, and specific to the individual.

In the case of organic brain disorders, it can only be a matter of getting the brain to function better rather than of curing the condition. When it comes to problems of deregulation, we would say that there is not a disease to be cured. Where deregulation is the problem, self-regulation may very well be the remedy. Again, the word cure would not apply.

Neurofeedback is especially concerned with the more "intractable" brain-based problems of childhood whose needs are not currently being met.

Many children have sleep problems that can be helped:

  • Headache/Migraine
We can also be helpful with many of the problems of adolescence:
  • Drug abuse
  • Suicidal behaviour
  • Anxiety and depression
Neurofeedbak can also help to maintain good brain function as people get older. The good news is that almost any brain, regardless of its level of function, can be trained to function better.

If the problem being addressed is one of brain deregulation, then the answer is yes, and that covers a lot of ground. Neurofeedback involves learning by the brain and if that brings order out of disorder, the brain will continue to use its new capabilities, and thus reinforce them.
Matters are different when we are dealing with degenerative conditions like Parkinson's or the dementias, or when we are working against continuing insults to the system, as may be the case in the autism spectrum. In such cases the training needs to be continued at some level over time.

Through the years of experience with Neurofeedback / EEG Biofeedback has reached the point of having very high expectations for success in training. When such success is not forthcoming, or if the gains cannot hold, then there is usually a reason for that which needs to be pursued. In the normal course of events, Neurofeedback ought to work with everybody. That is to say, nearly everyone should make gains that they themselves would judge to be worthwhile. Our brains are made for learning and skill-acquisition. On the other hand, we are working with many families whose expectations have been lowered by their past experience. And they need to see progress before they will share our optimism. It turns out that among the vast majority of clients (>95% in one clinician's experience) the actual outcome exceeds the prior expectations. Against such low expectations, the changes that can be produced with Neurofeedback may even appear miraculous.

With successful Neurofeedback / EEG Biofeedback training, the medications targeting brain function may very well no longer be needed, or they may be needed at lower dosages, as the brain takes over more of the role of regulating itself. This decrease in medications is particularly striking when the medications play a supportive role in any event, as is often the case for the more severe disorders that we are targeting with our work. It is important for clients to communicate with their prescribing physician regarding Neurofeedback / EEG Biofeedback and medications
